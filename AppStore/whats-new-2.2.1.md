# What's New — 2.2.1 (iOS build 27, Android code 22)

iPhone, iPad and Android. Everything since 2.2.0.

## English

New:
• Reading marker: a gold ribbon on the edge of the page. Interrupted? Drag it to the line you stopped at — it snaps line by line — and pick up from exactly there later. It works in all three reading modes (Madani page, flowing mushaf, ayah by ayah) and they all agree on the same line.
• Tap the faded ribbon on any other page to jump back to your marker. You can also place or remove it from an ayah's long-press menu.
• Continue Reading on Today opens straight at the marker, and its play button recites from there.
• Android: long-pressing a Madani line with more than one ayah lets you choose which ayah, and the ayah you arrive at from search, a bookmark or a juz is highlighted.
