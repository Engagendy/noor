# What's New — 2.1.0 (iOS build 23 · Android code 20)

Everything since 2.0.2. Both platforms carry the same features this time.

## English

New in this version:
• Share a passage, not just one ayah: choose how many ayat to include and they go on one card — and into one video, recited straight through in your reciter's voice. Short ayat finally make a video worth posting.
• Share the voluntary prayers: every nawafil — the rawatib, Duha, Qiyam al-Layl, Witr, the mosque greeting, after wudu — can be sent as a card with its rak'ahs, its time and its evidence, ready for a status.
• Continue Reading now has a play button: start the recitation from the page you stopped reading on, without opening the reader first.
• The poster in Settings → Share Noor now carries both the App Store and Google Play, so whoever you send it to can install Noor on the phone they actually have.

On the lock screen:
• The progress bar now runs across the whole surah instead of restarting every few seconds, and you can drag it to move through the surah.
• The ayah being recited is shown as the title, so you can follow the words with the screen locked.

Reading (iPhone and iPad):
• Opening a surah now brings its first ayah to the top of the page. Most surahs begin halfway down, under the end of the previous one, and it used to leave you hunting for where it started. The same applies when you arrive from search, a bookmark or a juz.
• When a page carries two surahs, the title bar names the one you opened or are listening to, not whichever one the page happens to begin with.
• The back arrow in the ayah actions sheet sits where it belongs again.

Fixed:
• Pausing while an ayah was still downloading is now respected — it no longer starts playing anyway a moment later. On Android, tapping play during that wait could close the app.
• Stopping a recitation just as it was starting no longer lets it begin regardless.
• Changing the reciter in the middle of a recitation no longer leaves two voices playing at once.
• Playing an athkar recording no longer skips the recitation forward by an ayah.
• On iPhone and iPad, the app could close immediately when opened by tapping a prayer notification.
