**Promotional text (max 170 chars):**

**Keywords (max 100 chars):**

**Description:**
Noor is a complete companion for your day of worship — the Quran, prayer times, and remembrance in one calm, beautiful app. Free forever, with no ads, no accounts, and no tracking of any kind.

THE QURAN, AS PRINTED
• The full Madani mushaf, page by page, exactly as printed — 604 pixel-perfect pages
• Flowing reading mode and ayah-by-ayah mode with translation
• Word-by-word mode with meanings, and tajweed guide
• Powerful search by word, phrase, or verse reference (like 2:255)
• Khatmah planner: finish the Quran in 15, 30, 60, or 90 days with a daily portion
• Bookmarks and reading position synced privately through your own iCloud

RECITATION
• 31 renowned reciters, from Al-Husary and Al-Minshawi to As-Sudais and Al-Afasy
• Word-by-word follow-along highlighting as the reciter reads
• Continuous playback across surahs, repeat modes, and a memorization loop
• Every ayah is cached after first play — listen offline

PRAYER
• Accurate prayer times for your city, fully offline, with multiple calculation methods
• Adhan notifications with beautiful, authentic recordings
• Qibla compass, hijri calendar, sunnah fasting reminders, and Ramadan countdowns

REMEMBRANCE
• Hisn al-Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim) with counters
• Morning, evening, and sleep athkar that follow your actual prayer times
• Tasbih counter, the 99 Names of Allah, and daily hadith from An-Nawawi's Forty
• Share any ayah, dhikr, or hadith as a beautiful image

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your location never leaves your device. There are no analytics, no ads, and no third-party services. Everything works offline after the initial content download.

Quran text from Tanzil (Uthmani). Prayer calculations by the adhan library. Recitations courtesy of EveryAyah. Full attributions inside the app.
