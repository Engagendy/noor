# What's New — 2.1.1 (build 24)

iPhone and iPad only. Everything since 2.1.0.

## English

• The reading mode panel now lets you pick the translation itself, not just switch it on — the same list as Settings, so the two always agree.
• The bottom bar no longer stays over the page after you leave the Quran tab and come back.
• While a recitation plays in Mushaf mode, the page follows it and keeps the ayah being recited in view.
